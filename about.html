About Me

Passionate About Sound, Dedicated to the Craft

I am Bronson Castillo, a freelance audio engineer, sound designer, and music producer. I partner with pop artists, indie filmmakers, and contemporary fashion and sports brands to deliver immersive, intentional sonic experiences.

My approach is built on three core pillars: absolute vocal clarity, inventive sound design, and an uncompromising eye for the micro-details that define a professional mix.

I don't rely on the newest or most complex plugins; I focus entirely on making individual elements work harmoniously together to serve the story. Let’s collaborate to give your next project the precision, clarity, and creative edge it deserves.
